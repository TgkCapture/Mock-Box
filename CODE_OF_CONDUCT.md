# Code of Conduct

Mock-Box follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short:

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever
their age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Respect differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and any other place
where you represent the project.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer,
[@TgkCapture](https://github.com/TgkCapture), using the contact details on their GitHub profile.
All reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential. The maintainer may remove, edit or
reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behavior is harmful,
following the [Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
